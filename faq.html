Frequently asked questions

Common questions from students about courses, exams, certificates, and study support.

Do I need to start from the beginning?

No. Our placement test helps match you with the right starting level, especially for IT and HSK courses.

Are the tests timed?

Some practice quizzes are untimed, while mock exams and HSK tests include countdown timers for realistic exam practice.

Can I download lesson notes?

Yes. Selected lesson notes and study sheets are available through the resources page and course sections.

How do I earn a certificate?

Finish the required lessons and pass the final assessment for the course to unlock your certificate.

Is AI allowed in the platform?

Yes, but students are encouraged to use AI responsibly, with critical thinking and original study effort.

What support is available?

Students can contact the team through the contact form, WhatsApp, and the course discussion channels.
