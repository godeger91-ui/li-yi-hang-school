Privacy policy

This platform respects student privacy and protects personal information used for learning support.

We collect account information needed to provide personalized learning features, including course progress, test scores, and contact details used for support communication.

Information is used to improve the learning experience, track progress, and administer certificates, announcements, and course recommendations.

We do not sell personal student data. Data is stored securely and only used for platform administration and learning support.
