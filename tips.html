🍅 Pomodoro

Study 25 minutes, rest 5. Four rounds, then a long break. Keeps your brain fresh and prevents burnout during long study sessions.

🔁 Spaced Repetition

Review each chapter again after 1 day, 3 days, then 1 week. Each review makes the memory more permanent.

✍️ Active Recall

Don't just re-read — close the book and try to write the code from memory. That's what the quiz section trains!

🛠️ Build Projects

Make a personal homepage, a class timetable page, a photo gallery. Real projects teach 10× faster than reading.

🌍 English Practice

Reading documentation in English (like W3Schools) improves coding and language skills at the same time.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Study Together

Explain each chapter to a friend — teaching is the fastest way to learn. Teamwork between God Eger & Li Yi Hang 💪.
